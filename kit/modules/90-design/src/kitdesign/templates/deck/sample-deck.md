# Support staff spend 40 % of their time on routine first replies

- 1,200 tickets per month, most answered from five standard cases
- First reply takes 12 minutes on average
- Customers wait 1.5 days for a first answer

# Drafts cut the time per first reply in a dry run

**12 → 5 min**

Measured on 60 synthetic tickets, every draft checked by a person

- Drafts a reply from the ticket and the knowledge base
- Marks sources it used
