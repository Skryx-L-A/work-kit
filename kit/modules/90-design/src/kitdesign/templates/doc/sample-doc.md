## Question

Can AI-drafted first replies save time without lowering answer quality?

## Method

One team used drafts for four weeks. Every draft was reviewed before sending. We compared
reply time and quality ratings with the four weeks before.

| Measure | Before | Pilot |
|---|--:|--:|
| First reply time (min) | 12.1 | 5.2 |
| Drafts accepted | n/a | 81 % |
| Quality rating (1–5) | 4.2 | 4.3 |

## Result

Reply time fell by more than half. Quality ratings did not drop.

## Next steps

1. Extend to two teams for six weeks.
2. Add the three most frequent rejection reasons to the draft instructions.
