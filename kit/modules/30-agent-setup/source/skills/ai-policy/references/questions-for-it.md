# Questions for IT, data protection and management (first weeks)

Starting point for the meeting that turns the `TODO(ask IT)` placeholders into rules. Run
`ai-gov open-questions` for the current, complete list from the installed files.

## Approved systems and data

1. Which AI systems are approved for company work, under which contracts, for which data
   classes? Where is the authoritative list?
2. Is there a data processing agreement for each; where is data processed and stored; is
   training on our inputs switched off?
3. May CONFIDENTIAL data be processed with local models on the laptop? Which model sources
   are accepted?
4. May customer code or data ever be used with AI? Which customer contracts allow it, and
   who checks per project?

## Roles and processes

5. Who owns the AI usage guideline, who approves new AI tools and MCP servers, and how are
   requests submitted?
6. Where are the AI system inventory, the MCP allowlist and the approvals kept officially?
7. Who receives AI incident reports; how do they connect to the ISMS incident process?
8. Is there an AI management system (ISO/IEC 42001) or is AI governance part of the ISMS?

## People and transparency

9. Which role is the contact for the works council on AI tools that could monitor staff?
10. How are AI literacy measures (EU AI Act Art. 4) organised and recorded?
11. Labeling rule for AI-generated content in customer deliverables and publications?
12. Rules for AI meeting assistants: consent, recording, transcripts, retention?

## Agents

13. Which coding agents / harnesses are approved, and with which settings (auto-approve,
    sandbox, network)?
14. Are MCP servers allowed at all; is there a gateway or a central registry?
15. Review interval for the allowlist and inventory?
