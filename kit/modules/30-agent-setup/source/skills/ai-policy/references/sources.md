  https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/faqs/ai-literacy-questions-answers
- European Commission, AI Act Service Desk, implementation timeline:
  https://ai-act-service-desk.ec.europa.eu/en/ai-act/timeline/timeline-implementation-eu-ai-act

  Art. 50 (transparency), Annex III (high-risk purposes): https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj

  reproduced): https://www.iso.org/standard/81230.html
- GDPR Art. 33 (72-hour breach notification): https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj
