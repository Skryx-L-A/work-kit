# Slide layout rules and checklist

## Rules

- 16:9 unless the room or template says otherwise.
- Grid: fixed margins (about 5 % of width), titles always at the same position.
- Title 28 to 36 pt, body at least 18 pt, labels in charts at least 14 pt. If it does not
  fit at 18 pt, split the slide.
- Two type levels plus a small label style. One font family (two at most).
- Colour: one accent for the key element, the rest neutral. Contrast at least 4.5:1;
  projectors wash out light greys and thin lines.
- Charts: title states the finding, direct labels instead of legends, highlight the one
  series that matters.
- Diagrams: left to right or top to bottom, at most 7 boxes, arrows labelled with what flows.
- Screenshots: crop to the relevant area, enlarge, add one callout.
- Numbers: round to what matters (38 %, not 37.84 %), units always shown, source line small.
- Footer: slide number; date and data-class label if required; no logo on every slide
  unless the template demands it.

## Checklist per slide (on the rendered PDF)

1. Title is a full-sentence message.
2. Nothing smaller than 14 pt; nothing touching the edge.
3. At most one idea; at most 5 bullet lines of at most one line each.
4. Charts readable from the back of the room; labels not overlapping.
5. Consistent positions of title, body and footer across slides.
6. No leftover template text, speaker notes visible, or broken images.
7. No customer names, personal data or internal hostnames.
