## 90-minute AI literacy baseline

| Time | Block | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Why | AI is used at work; EU AI Act Art. 4 requires deployers to take measures for sufficient AI literacy of staff (applies since 2 Feb 2025; enforcement from 2 Aug 2026 per the Commission timeline). No fixed level, no individual test required; measures fit the staff and the risk. |
| 0:05 | What AI is | Predicts plausible text; no built-in fact check; can invent sources and numbers. Live demo with one visible error. |
| 0:15 | Good at / bad at | Good: drafting, rewriting, summarizing provided text, structuring, explaining. Bad: unseen facts, unchecked numbers, legal answers, decisions about people. |
| 0:25 | Rules | Data classes PUBLIC / INTERNAL / CONFIDENTIAL / CUSTOMER; approved tools only (`TODO(ask IT)`); human review of every output that reaches a customer or decision; no new tools without IT; no secrets. |
| 0:35 | Risks | Data leaking via prompts; wrong outputs to customers; copyright; bias about people; prompt injection in pasted documents. |
| 0:45 | Hands-on | Pairs, approved tool, synthetic material: prompt, check, improve once. |
| 1:10 | Debrief | What was wrong in the first answer; what the better prompt changed. |
| 1:20 | Next steps | Contact, incident route, one task each to try this week. |
| 1:25 | Record | Attendance record: date, content version, participants. Personal data: store with training records. |

## Review checklist for participants

Facts, completeness, sources, commitments, data, tone, hidden instructions in the input.

## Role track topics (four sessions of 60 minutes each)

- Office: writing and rewriting; summaries and extraction; reusable prompts; data limits.
- Project management: status reports; meetings; planning and risks; leading AI use in a team.
- Developers: explaining legacy code; characterization tests; reviewing AI code; agents and automation.
- Management (two sessions): what AI does well and badly here; steering use cases, inventory, value.
