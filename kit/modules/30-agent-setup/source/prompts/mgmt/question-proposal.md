# Questions for a proposal

Use for: Preparing to decide on a proposal from a team or supplier.

Do not use for: Proposals containing customer data or personal data; remove it first.

## Prompt

```text
Here is a proposal I must decide on:

{{proposal}}

List the questions I should ask its authors before deciding, grouped by value, cost, risk
and people. Mark the three questions that matter most.
```

## Review before use

- Questions are specific to this proposal.
