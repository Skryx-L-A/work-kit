# AI literacy record (EU AI Act Art. 4)

One record per session or measure. The Act asks for measures that fit role, knowledge and
the systems used; it does not require tests or certificates. The record shows what was done.
Keep records where `TODO(ask IT)` says; do not keep them in a personal tool.
