# Answer key (facilitators only)

## Part A

1. (1) It generates plausible text from patterns; it does not look facts up or know when it
   is wrong, unless it is given the source. Accept any answer with "predicts/plausible" and
   "no built-in fact check".
2. (2, half a point each) a) PUBLIC b) CUSTOMER c) CONFIDENTIAL d) INTERNAL.
3. (1) Third option. Removing names alone does not make a log anonymous (ids, paths, error
   texts can still identify the customer), and unapproved tools are never allowed.
4. (2, one point each, any two) Facts and numbers against the source; names and addresses;
   commitments or promises the mail makes; tone and politeness; no internal or confidential
   content included; attachments and links correct.
5. (1) Ranking job applicants and scoring employees. Both are decisions about people (high
   risk under the EU AI Act; employee monitoring also touches works-council and data
   protection rules). Both boxes needed for the point.
6. (1) Prompt injection: text in the input tries to steer the AI. Do not follow it, do not let
   an agent act on that document, tell the contact person if an agent had tool access.
7. (1) Correct it with the recipient and tell your lead; report through the incident route if
   data or a customer is affected. Accept "correct and inform".
8. (1) The person who hands it in.

## Part B scoring

| Criterion | Passed when |
|---|---|
| Prompt | States task, context (what the input is, who the audience is) and output format |
| Improvement | Second prompt fixes a concrete problem of the first answer |
| Result | Could be used after at most small edits |
| Checks | Refer to the source (e.g. "action item 3 was missing", "date was wrong") |
| Data rules | Only the exercise material or cleared data was used, in an approved tool |

"Not yet" is not a failure: agree one follow-up task and recheck in the next session.
