# AI enablement pack

Material to run AI training for staff who use AI at work, most of them without a technical
background. Everything is plain Markdown: edit it, then turn it into slides or PDF.

## Before the first session

1. Fill every `TODO(ask IT)` in `handouts/data-classes-card.md` and in
   `art4-literacy-session.md` with the company's real values: approved tools, allowed data
   per tool, contact for questions and incidents. Until then, teach the strict default.
2. Agree the owner of the program (who signs off material and keeps attendance records).
3. Replace the synthetic examples in `exercises/` only with material that is cleared for the
   tool used in the session. Never use customer data in a training.

## What is in here

| File | Use it for |
|---|---|
| `facilitator-guide.md` | How to prepare, run and follow up a session; room, timing, questions, difficult moments |
| `curriculum.md` | Role-based tracks: office staff, project managers, developers, management |
| `art4-literacy-session.md` | 90-minute baseline session every person gets (EU AI Act Art. 4 literacy) |
| `train-the-trainer.md` | Track for AI champions who will run sessions in their own team |
| `assessment/completion-assessment.md` | Short knowledge check plus practical task for participants |
| `assessment/answer-key.md` | Answers and scoring for facilitators only |
| `handouts/starter-prompt-pack.md` | Prompts people can use on day one, per role |
| `handouts/*.md` | One-pagers and slide outlines to hand out or present |
| `templates/*.md` | Attendance and literacy record, feedback form, adoption metrics, program plan |
| `exercises/*.md` | Synthetic practice material per role |
| `faq.md` | Answers to the questions and objections that come up in every session |

## Program shape

1. Everyone: `art4-literacy-session.md` (90 min). Record attendance with
   `templates/literacy-record.md`.
2. Role track from `curriculum.md`: 3 to 4 short sessions, one per week, each with a task
   people do in their real work before the next session.
3. Assessment at the end of the track; people who pass get a completion note.
4. Champions from each team take `train-the-trainer.md` and run the next cohort.
5. Every 4 to 6 weeks: collect `templates/adoption-metrics.md` and update the material.

## Principles

- AI only for well-defined tasks. Every output that leaves your hands is reviewed by you.
- Classify data before you paste it anywhere. When unsure, ask before you send.
- Practice on the tasks people actually do, with the tools the company actually approved.
- Measure use and time saved honestly, including the time spent checking outputs.

The pack is not legal advice. Have the data protection officer or legal review the Art. 4
session and the literacy record before the first run.
