# Using AI at work — the one-page version

**Our line.** We use AI for well-defined tasks. A person checks every result that reaches a
customer or informs a decision, and that person is responsible for it.

**Good uses.** Drafting and rewriting text; summarizing documents you provide; extracting
action items; structuring notes; explaining code or concepts; brainstorming questions and
risks; first drafts of tests and documentation.

**Poor uses.** Facts the tool cannot see; numbers without checking; legal or contractual
answers; anything decided about people (hiring, performance, promotion); sending anything
without reading it.

**Data rules.** Classify before you paste: PUBLIC, INTERNAL, CONFIDENTIAL, CUSTOMER. Only
approved tools, only for the classes they are approved for (`TODO(ask IT)`). No passwords,
keys or tokens, ever. Customer contracts can be stricter than our rules.

**No shadow tools.** No personal accounts, free AI chats, plugins or browser extensions for
work data. New tools go through IT.

**Checking.** Facts, completeness, sources, commitments, data, tone. Text you pasted may
contain hidden instructions; stay suspicious of surprising results.

**Problems.** Wrong result already sent: correct it and tell your lead. Data sent to the wrong
place: report at once to `TODO(ask IT)`. Reporting early is always right.
