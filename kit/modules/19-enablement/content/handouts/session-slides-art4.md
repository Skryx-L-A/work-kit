# Why we are here

- We use AI tools at work, and customers expect us to use them competently
- The EU AI Act (Art. 4) asks every company that uses AI to build AI literacy
- Today: what AI does, our rules, and one real task done together

# What a language model does

- Predicts plausible text from patterns and from what you give it
- Does not look facts up, does not know our company, does not know today
- Can invent sources, numbers and names in a confident tone
- Works best on material you provide

# Good at, bad at

| Good at | Bad at |
|---|---|
| Drafting and rewriting | Facts it cannot see |
| Summarizing your text | Calculations without checking |
| Structuring, explaining | Legal or contract answers |
| Brainstorming questions | Decisions about people |

# Our rules

1. Classify before you paste: PUBLIC, INTERNAL, CONFIDENTIAL, CUSTOMER
2. Only approved tools, only for approved classes (`TODO(ask IT)`)
3. A person checks every result that reaches a customer or a decision
4. No new tools, plugins or accounts without IT
5. No passwords, keys or tokens, ever

# Risks in practice

- Data leaves the company through a prompt or an upload
- A wrong answer reaches a customer
- Generated text or code copies someone else's work
- Biased output about people
- Hidden instructions inside a document you paste

# Hands-on

- Pairs, 25 minutes, approved tool, synthetic material
- Write a prompt, check the answer with the review card
- Improve the prompt once; note what changed

# Check before you use it

Facts, completeness, sources, commitments, data, tone, hidden instructions

- Wrong result sent: correct it, tell your lead
