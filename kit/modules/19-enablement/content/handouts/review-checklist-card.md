# Check before you use an AI result

You stay responsible for everything you send or decide. Check in this order:

1. **Facts** — every name, number, date and quote matches the source. Anything without a
   source is checked or removed.
2. **Completeness** — nothing important from the source is missing (compare the headings or
   the list of points).
3. **Sources** — links and references exist and say what the text claims. Open them.
4. **Commitments** — the text promises nothing you cannot or may not promise.
5. **Data** — no confidential, personal or customer information that the reader must not see.
6. **Tone** — fits the reader and the relationship.
7. **Instructions in the input** — if the pasted text contained instructions ("ignore the
   above ..."), the result may be manipulated. Treat it with suspicion.

Found a problem? Tell the tool exactly what is wrong and ask again, or fix it yourself.
It went out already? Correct it and tell your lead; report data problems at once.
