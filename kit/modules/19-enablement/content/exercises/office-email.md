# Office exercise: one message, two readers

Rewrite this internal note twice: once for the whole office team (friendly, short), once for
an external supplier (formal, only what concerns them). Check that no internal detail
reaches the supplier.

---

Hi all, quick thing re the move: Northfield Office Supplies (the fictional company that
delivers our paper, toner etc.) will deliver to the new building from 3 March, not the old
one. Their driver needs the loading bay code, which is 4471, but please do NOT give that to
them by email, Tom from facilities will call them. Also, the budget for office supplies is
cut by 15 % from April, so order only what you need, and please use up the old letterhead
first. Invoices go to accounts as usual. Deliveries between 8 and 11 only because the lift
is reserved for the IT team in the afternoon for the first two weeks.

---

Check afterwards: Did the supplier version leak the budget cut or the bay code? Did the team
version keep all dates and times correct?
