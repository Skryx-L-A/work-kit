# Data sort: send, anonymize first, or never

For each card decide: may this go into the approved tool as it is, only after anonymizing,
or never? Assume the default rules (approved tool: PUBLIC yes, INTERNAL ask, CONFIDENTIAL
and CUSTOMER no) unless your company table says otherwise.

1. The text of our public job advertisement.
2. A colleague's sick-leave note.
3. The agenda of an internal all-hands meeting.
4. A customer's complaint email with name and contract number.
5. Our price list for customers.
6. A password you forgot and want to "check".
7. A template letter without any names.
8. The minutes of a works-council meeting.
9. An anonymized example you invented to show a process.
10. A spreadsheet of employee salaries.
11. A published press release of a customer.
12. A screenshot of a customer's system with an error message.

Facilitator notes: send 1, 7, 9, 11; 3 only if the tool is approved for INTERNAL.
Never 2, 6, 8, 10 (personal, secret, confidential). Price list 5: CONFIDENTIAL unless it is
published. Customer items 4 and 12: CUSTOMER; anonymizing a single email or screenshot
reliably is hard (contract numbers, system names, layout), so the default answer is "no".
