# Three AI outputs to judge (synthetic)

For each output decide: send as is, fix first, or reject. Say why.

**Output 1: reply to a customer asking about a delay**
"Dear Mr. Example, thank you for your patience. We are pleased to confirm that the
remaining features will be delivered on 15 May at no additional cost, and we have added two
senior developers to your project."
(Facts: no date was agreed; staffing is not decided; cost is under discussion.)

**Output 2: summary of an internal cost report for the management meeting**
"Cloud costs rose 12 % quarter on quarter, mainly from the test environments that ran over
weekends. Switching them off on weekends would save about 8 % of the total."
(Facts: the rise was 12 %; the report attributes it to test environments; the 8 % figure is
not in the report.)

**Output 3: ranking of internal applicants for a team lead role, based on their CVs**
"Candidate B is the strongest fit, followed by A and C."

Facilitator notes: 1 reject or rewrite (invented commitments to a customer). 2 fix: remove or
source the 8 % figure. 3 reject: a decision about people, not a use for AI here, and likely a
high-risk use under the EU AI Act (employment).
