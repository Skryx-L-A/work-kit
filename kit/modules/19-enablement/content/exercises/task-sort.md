# Task sort: good fit, careful, not with AI

Put each card on one of three piles. Discuss the cards where the group disagrees.

1. Rewrite a long internal email so it is shorter and friendlier.
2. Tell me the current VAT rules for a customer invoice.
3. Summarize a 20-page specification that I paste in.
4. Choose which of three applicants to invite.
5. Suggest questions to ask a customer in a kickoff meeting.
6. Calculate the total project budget from a list of 40 line items.
7. Explain what a piece of old code does, as a starting point for my own reading.
8. Answer a customer's support ticket and send the reply automatically.

Facilitator notes: good fit 1, 3, 5, 7 (you provide the material and check the result).
Careful 2 and 6: facts and numbers must be checked against an authoritative source or a
spreadsheet. Not with AI: 4 (decision about people) and 8 (no human review before a customer).
